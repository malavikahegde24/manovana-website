## What it is

Resentment is heavy. It quietly drains energy, colours relationships, and keeps old wounds alive. Forgiveness Therapy is the guided process of setting that weight down, reclaiming the peace that unforgiveness has been holding hostage.

## How it works

Through reflective and emotional-release practices, you acknowledge the hurt honestly, loosen its grip, and consciously choose to let it go. Forgiveness here is an act of self-liberation: you stop paying rent on a pain you never deserved.

## How Malavika works with you

Malavika gently guides you to release emotional baggage that ordinary talk-therapy may not have cleared, including resentment toward others and, often most powerfully, toward yourself.
