## What it is

Much of what drives us lives below conscious awareness, in the subconscious mind. Hypnotherapy uses a state of deep, focused relaxation to communicate directly with that deeper layer — where lasting change is often easiest to make.

## How it works

In a calm, guided state, the usual mental chatter quietens and the subconscious becomes more open. From here, old patterns and limiting beliefs can be gently released and healthier ones suggested in their place. You remain fully aware and in control the entire time.

Malavika uses hypnotherapy to help you reach and reshape the subconscious roots of stress, fear and self-sabotage — change that conscious willpower alone often can't reach.
