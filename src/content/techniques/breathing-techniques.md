## What it is

Your breath is the one part of the nervous system you can consciously steer. Breathing techniques use deliberate rhythms of inhale, hold and exhale to shift your body out of "fight or flight" and into calm — no app, no equipment, just you.

## How it works

When you slow and lengthen the exhale, you signal safety to the nervous system. The body follows: heart rate settles, muscles soften, and the storm of anxious thinking begins to quieten. With practice these techniques become an instant reset you can reach for anywhere.
