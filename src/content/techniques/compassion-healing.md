## What it is

Many people are kinder to everyone but themselves. Compassion Healing turns that kindness inward — softening the relentless inner critic and meeting your own struggles with warmth instead of judgement.

## How it works

Through guided practices, you learn to relate to your pain, mistakes and fears with gentleness rather than attack. This shift calms the nervous system, dissolves shame, and creates the safe inner ground on which all other healing grows.

## How Malavika works with you

Malavika helps you build a compassionate relationship with yourself — essential for anyone "doing well on the outside but broken inside." From self-kindness, clarity and confidence naturally follow.
