## What it is

So much of adult struggle traces back to early wounds, moments where we learned, often unconsciously, that we were not safe, not enough, or not lovable. Inner child healing gently addresses these roots rather than only managing the symptoms.

## How it works

Through compassionate, paced inner work, you reconnect with and soothe the younger parts of yourself that still carry hurt. As those wounds heal, the present-day patterns they fuelled, anxiety, people-pleasing, relationship struggles, begin to loosen.

## How Malavika works with you

Malavika holds a safe, non-judgemental space to heal the pain you may have carried for years, especially for those who "display happiness and success on the outside while carrying loads of pain from the inside."
