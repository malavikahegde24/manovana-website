## What it is

Emotional Freedom Technique, often called "tapping", combines focused attention on a feeling with gentle tapping on specific points on the body. It is a simple, body-based way to take the charge out of stress, anxiety and distressing emotions.

## How it works

By tapping while tuning into an emotion, you help the nervous system settle and the emotional intensity subside. Issues that felt overwhelming often soften noticeably within a single round, making space for calmer thinking.
