## What is a Mind Coach?

A **Mind Coach** helps you work directly with the inner world, your thoughts, emotions, beliefs and subconscious patterns, to heal old pain, break free of limiting habits, and build a steady sense of inner peace. Where much of life focuses on changing your circumstances, a Mind Coach helps you change your relationship with your own mind.

> *"Mind is everything. What you think, you become."*

## What does a Mind Coach actually do?

A Mind Coach blends proven techniques to meet you where you are. With Malavika Hegde, that holistic blend includes **meditation and mindfulness, breathing techniques, NLP, EFT, hypnotherapy, forgiveness and gratitude therapy, and more**. Together these help you:

- Release stress, anxiety and emotional tension
- Heal childhood wounds and old resentment
- Break limiting beliefs and self-sabotaging patterns
- Rediscover clarity, confidence and joy

## Who is mind coaching for?

Mind coaching is for **anyone aged 18 to 80**, working professionals, homemakers, and retired individuals alike. It is especially powerful for people who are *"doing well on the outside but broken inside"*, carrying a quiet emptiness, mood swings, or emotional baggage that ordinary approaches haven't cleared.
