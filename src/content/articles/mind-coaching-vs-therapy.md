## A common, fair question

If you're considering working on your inner life, it's natural to ask how **mind coaching** differs from **therapy**. Both care about your wellbeing, but they approach it differently.

## How they differ

**Therapy** (counselling, clinical psychology, psychiatry) is often oriented toward diagnosing and treating mental-health conditions, frequently working with the past and with clinical frameworks.

**Mind coaching** is a **growth-focused, forward-looking practice**. Rather than treating illness, it helps you release emotional baggage, dissolve limiting beliefs, and build clarity, confidence and inner peace, using practical tools like meditation, NLP, EFT and hypnotherapy.

## They're not rivals, they're partners

The two approaches complement each other beautifully. Mind coaching is not a replacement for medical or psychological treatment. For diagnosed conditions, Malavika Hegde works **alongside** appropriate professional care, never instead of it.

## Which is right for you?

If you're carrying emotional baggage that *"therapy hasn't cleared,"* feeling stuck despite outward success, or simply wanting to grow into a calmer, more fulfilled version of yourself, mind coaching may be exactly the support you're looking for.
