## Connection that nourishes, not drains

Relationship pain — resentment, repeating conflicts, entangled bonds — can quietly shape your whole sense of wellbeing. Often the same patterns repeat across different people, which is a sign the roots lie within and can be healed.

## How Malavika helps

Through **forgiveness therapy**, **karmic cleansing** and **childhood-trauma healing**, Malavika helps you release old resentment and break the cycles that keep replaying — so you can heal existing bonds and build healthier, more fulfilling connections.
