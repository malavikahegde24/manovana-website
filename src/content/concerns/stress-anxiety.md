## You don't have to live on edge

Constant stress and anxiety keep the body braced and the mind racing. Over time that takes a quiet toll on your sleep, relationships, focus and joy. The good news: calm is a skill that can be learned.

## How Malavika helps

Through **proven mindfulness techniques**, conscious breathing and EFT, you learn to release tension and find calm on demand, while deeper work settles the patterns that keep anxiety alive. As one participant shared, the sessions offered *"practical techniques to manage stress and emotions."*

You'll move from being ruled by stress to meeting it with steadiness.
